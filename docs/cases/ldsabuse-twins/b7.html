\(B_7\) and the Lettering of the Twin Structure

On the X / Instagram twins of @ldsabuse and ldsabuseonx · October 11, 2026 · not yet peer reviewed
Abstract. An X post and an Instagram post are twins if they carry the same media within three days. The rule was fixed and agreed on X by both sides (John and Grok, with Claude) before any search. Among ldsabuseonx’s own posts there are seven twin pairs. Six were found first; the seventh, \(B_7\), was found last because it is the only pair more than 33 minutes apart: 47 h 53 min. We model the pairs as an incidence structure \(\mathcal S_{\rm twin}\) and show:
  • lettering the blocks by date is the relabelling \(\sigma=(5\ 6\ 7)\), and it puts the incidence matrix in row, column order, \(N=\binom{I_7}{I_7}\);
  • relabelling does not change the automorphism group, \(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal S_{\rm twin})\cong S_2\wr S_7\).
The pre-registered prediction is then visible in the matrix itself.

0Notation

As in the companion paper (Automorphism Group of the Incidence Structure and Its Orbits), points are written in lower case and blocks as \(B_i\). Here the points are posts, not accounts, and \(\mathcal S_{\rm twin}\) is a different structure from that paper’s \(\mathcal S_7\). A block is a set of points; a relabelling is a permutation. So \(B_7\) is a block (a pair of posts), and the 3-cycle below is the relabelling \(\sigma\) that moves its label. \(B_7\) is not itself a 3-cycle; our own X reply of October 11 says so, correcting an earlier post of ours that blurred the two.

1Setting

Definition 1.1 (twin rule, fixed October 8, 2026 before the search). An Instagram post \(g\) of ldsabuseonx and an X post \(x\) of @ldsabuse are twins if \[|t(g)-t(x)|\le 3\ \text{days}\quad\text{and}\quad d_H\big(h(g),h(x)\big)\le 5,\] where \(h\) is the 64-bit difference hash of the image or of video frames at 30/50/70%, after pure-black side bars are trimmed, and \(d_H\) is Hamming distance. Videos must also agree in length to within 0.2 s.
Definition 1.2. \(\mathcal S_{\rm twin}=(P,\mathcal B)\): the points \(P\) are the 14 posts that have a twin (7 on X, 7 on Instagram); each block \(B=\{x,g\}\) is one twin pair.

The rule was proposed in our X post 2108275591116177432 and confirmed by Grok 53 s later. John accepted it as @Jimzz1e. The amendment trimming black bars was proposed in 2108285718091256248 and accepted by Grok 40 s later. Both were recorded before the search, in 01_FALSIFY_PLAN.md.

3The lettering

Let \(r(B)\) be a block’s rank by date. Then \(r(B_i)=i\) for \(i\le4\), \(r(B_7)=5\), \(r(B_5)=6\), \(r(B_6)=7\). As a permutation of positions this is

\[\sigma=(5\ 6\ 7):\qquad 5\mapsto6,\ 6\mapsto7,\ 7\mapsto5,\]

a 3-cycle (an even permutation), not a transposition. \(\sigma\) is determined by upload times alone, so it is invariant: it does not depend on who found what first.

Proposition 3.1 (row, column order). Order the columns of \(N\) by \(r\). Order the rows as the seven X posts by date, then the seven Instagram posts by date. Then \[N=\begin{pmatrix}I_7\\ I_7\end{pmatrix}.\]
Each block contains exactly one X post and one Instagram post, and no post lies in two blocks, so every row of \(N\) has a single 1. The X posts in date order are the blocks in date order, and so are the Instagram posts: the Instagram times run Jul 31, Aug 3, 11, 12, 20, 24, 24 against X’s Jul 31, Aug 3, 11, 12, 18, 24, 24, in the same order. So the \(j\)th X row and the \(j\)th Instagram row both have their 1 in column \(j\). □

4Why the group survives relabelling

Lemma 4.1. Renaming the blocks leaves \(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal S)\le\operatorname{Sym}(P)\) unchanged.
\(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal S)=\{\pi:\pi(\mathcal B)=\mathcal B\}\) depends on \(\mathcal B\) as a set of point sets, not on the names of its members. A new set of labels permutes the columns of \(N\) (\(N\mapsto NQ\)) and defines the same \(\mathcal B\). □
Lemma 4.2. Renaming the points by \(\pi\in\operatorname{Sym}(P)\) gives \(\operatorname{Aut}(\pi\mathcal S)=\pi\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal S)\pi^{-1}\cong\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal S)\).
\(\tau\) preserves \(\pi\mathcal B\) iff \(\pi^{-1}\tau\pi\) preserves \(\mathcal B\). □

So the answer to “why do we end up with another group after the mapping?” is that we don’t, up to isomorphism. Moving labels of blocks changes nothing. Moving labels of points gives a conjugate subgroup, which is the same group abstractly.

Proposition 4.3. \(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal S_{\rm twin})\cong S_2\wr S_7\), of order \(2^7\cdot7!=645{,}120\). Its subgroup keeping each post on its own platform is \(\cong S_7\), of order 5,040. The only relabelling that also keeps date order is the identity.
The blocks are seven pairwise disjoint pairs covering \(P\). Swapping the two points of a pair, or permuting the pairs, preserves \(\mathcal B\), and nothing else does; that is \(S_2\wr S_7\) (the same group as the largest pendant-star factor in the companion paper’s Theorem 3.2). Fixing the platform forbids the swaps, leaving \(S_7\). Fixing date order as well forces each pair to its own rank, leaving the identity. □

So the date lettering is the unique lettering of \(\mathcal S_{\rm twin}\) that respects both platform and time. That is the sense in which \(\sigma\) is the invariant map.

5The seven in date order

Figure 1. One column per block, in date order, with the gap between the two posts of each twin. Circle = video, square = photo.

Which platform posted first plays no part in the rule or in the result. What matters is the gap. In six pairs the two posts are within 33 minutes of each other; in \(B_7\) they are 47 h 53 min apart, about 87 times the largest of the other gaps. It is also the only photo. That is why a same-day comparison found six and the 3-day rule found the seventh.

6The prediction and the result

Before the search (T4, October 8), the four own posts then without a twin were Jul 15, Aug 12 (Db9nwIFM1L7), Aug 16 and Aug 20. The predictions on record were:

Extend \(N\) to all of ldsabuseonx’s 13 own posts, adding the six posts with no twin as rows: the three unmatched (Jul 15, Aug 12, Aug 16) and the three with no file to score (two June 2024 posts and Mar 26). Lettered by date, with these rows last, the matrix is

\[N_{20\times7}=\begin{pmatrix}I_7\\ I_7\\ 0_{6\times7}\end{pmatrix}.\]

The prediction is legible in it. The four T4 posts appear as one identity row (Aug 20, in column 5, which is \(B_7\)) and three zero rows: at least two zero rows, as predicted.

7Scope

“Twin” means same media under Definition 1.1, nothing more. It does not say who runs either account, or whether the matches were made by one hand or two. Times are from post IDs, a few minutes from what the platforms display. The Mar 26 and June 2024 posts could not be scored, and the drive that held some copies failed on October 11, 2026. Data, scripts, custody log and the X record are in the mimesis case data/x_ig_ldsabuse_2026-10; the public case page is ldsabuse-twins.

Therefore, there exists a lettering in row, column order that fits our prediction.
